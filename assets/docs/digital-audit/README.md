# Digital Audit — Weber Workshops

A comprehensive digital audit for Weber Workshops, a specialty coffee equipment brand, covering SEO, paid media, organic social, and reporting opportunities — built around a simple frame: revenue = traffic × conversion rate × AOV, and every recommendation ties back to moving one of those three levers.

## What's in it

**Competitive benchmarking.** Domain authority, linking domains, and ranking keywords for Weber Workshops against category competitors (Fellow, La Marzocco, Mahlkönig, Niche Coffee) to size the gap and flag who's investing in content (most competitors run an active blog; Weber didn't at the time).

**Technical & on-page SEO.** Title tag rewrites to include target keywords within the ~65-character limit before truncation, header tag gaps, and a homepage image audit that found 9 of 19 images missing alt text. Confirmed sitemap and robots.txt were in place but not yet submitted to Google Search Console, and recommended adding an HTML sitemap in the footer (a tactic a competitor already used).

**Site speed.** Mobile PageSpeed score of 41 versus desktop's 77 — notable given roughly half of visits abandon a mobile site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load.

**Content depth.** Word-count comparison across the homepage and key product comparison pages (e.g., EG-1 vs. Ode, BIRD vs. Aiden) showing Weber running noticeably thinner than competitor equivalents, with product FAQs flagged as an SEO-friendly way to close the gap.

**Paid, organic social, and reporting.** Additional sections cover paid ad opportunities, organic social opportunities, and a custom reporting recommendation, rounding out the audit beyond organic search.
